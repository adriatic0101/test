About California

California, also known as the Golden State, is located on the west coast of the United States. It is famous for its diverse culture, beautiful landscapes, and thriving economy.

Key Highlights

  • Home to Silicon Valley, the hub of technology and innovation.
  • Famous landmarks like the Golden Gate Bridge and Hollywood.
  • Beautiful national parks such as Yosemite and Redwood.
  • Known for its sunny beaches and vibrant cities like Los Angeles and San Francisco.
